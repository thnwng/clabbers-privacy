Clabbers Privacy Policy

Applies to the Clabbers app (package dev.clabbers.app) and the Clabbers website. Effective 2026-08-15.

Clabbers is a word-study app. It is built to work offline, and it collects as little data as possible: no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account unless you choose to create one.

Data stored on your device

Your study data — word lists, flashcards, scheduling state, statistics, and app settings — is stored locally on your device. If you never sign in, none of it leaves your device.

Optional account and sync

You can optionally create an account (email address and password) to sync your study progress between devices. If you do:

  • Your email address and password are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. We never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Your study data (cards, scheduling state, word lists, settings, and statistics) is synced to and stored on the Clabbers server so your other signed-in devices can retrieve it.
  • This data is used only to provide the sync feature. It is never sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.

Contact form

If you use the in-app Contact page, we receive what you submit: the topic, your message, an optional reply email address, the platform you are on, and any screenshots you choose to attach. For bug reports, you can optionally tick a box to attach a diagnostics report — it contains technical information only (app version and build, platform type, sync status, and a log of recent in-app technical events), and it is sent only when you tick that box.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of your data, ever.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites.

Data retention and deleting your data

On-device data: delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

Account and synced data: to delete your account and all study data stored on our server, send a request from the in-app Contact page or email ethanwong27@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. We will delete the account and its synced data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Security

All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Account credentials are managed by Supabase's authentication service.

Children

Clabbers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
