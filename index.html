Clabbers Privacy Policy

Applies to the Clabbers apps for Android (package dev.clabbers.app), iOS, Windows and macOS, and to the Clabbers website at clabbers.app. Effective 2026-10-01.

Clabbers is an independent word-study app for competitive word-game players. This page explains, in plain terms, what information Clabbers handles, why, who else processes it for us, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

In short

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, no advertising ID, and we never sell your data.
  • The apps work without an account. Your study data stays on your device unless you sign in (a guest sign-in counts as signing in).
  • The website needs a sign-in, an account or a guest, because it keeps your study data on our server.
  • You can delete your account and everything our server keeps for it at any time, in the app, on the website, or by email.

1. Who we are

Clabbers is developed and published by Ethan Wong, an independent developer ("we" in this policy), who is responsible for how Clabbers handles your data. For any question about this policy or your data, or to make a request, email clabbersapp@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

The apps keep your study data in a database on your device: your cards and their scheduling state, your review history, decks, saved lists, notes you write on cards, study goals, game history and settings. If you never sign in, none of it leaves your device.

  • The on-device database is encoded with a key built into the app. That keeps it from being read casually, but it is not strong protection: your device's own lock screen and storage encryption are what protect it.
  • Study reminders on phones are local notifications. The app works out how many cards are due on the device; nothing is sent anywhere to schedule them. They are off until you turn them on.
  • Some lists, such as the words you missed in Boggle and Wordwalls, never leave the device even when you sign in.
  • If you import a word list or a file of your own, the app processes it on the device.

3. Accounts and sign-in

Signing in is optional in the apps and needed on the website. You can sign in with an email address and password, with Google, or as a guest ("Try it without an account").

  • Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. Your password goes only to Supabase, which stores it in hashed form; the Clabbers server never receives it.
  • Supabase stores your email address and how you sign in. If you use Google, Supabase also keeps the basic profile details Google shares at sign-in (your name, email address and profile picture link). We ask Google for nothing more than that.
  • A guest account is a real account with no email address. You can add an email and password to it later and keep what you have studied.
  • Our own database keeps, for each account: a random account ID, the sign-in ID from Supabase, when the account was created, and your profile: an optional display name, whether you want reminder emails, and your chosen reminder hour and time zone (the website saves your time zone when you save the account settings, even with reminders off).
  • On a device, the app remembers which profile belongs to which account and labels it with your display name or email address.

4. What our server keeps when you sign in

When you are signed in (as an account or a guest), your study data is kept on our server so that your devices and the website can share it. It includes:

  • your cards and their scheduling state, and your full review history (when you reviewed each card, how you rated it, and how many answers you got or missed);
  • decks (names, folders, descriptions and the search they are built from), saved lists, notes you write on cards, study goals and study activity;
  • your settings (appearance, study options and similar preferences);
  • Wordwalls and Boggle game history;
  • records of what you deleted, so the deletion reaches your other devices; a deleted card can be restored for about 30 days;
  • a random ID for each installation of the app, used only to keep each device's changes apart when they sync;
  • Daily puzzles: when you started, the words you entered or that were accepted, your scores, times, streaks and days played;
  • Versus: the results of your matches (each player's display name, score and placing);
  • on the website only, for accounts with an email address (not guests): a short technical log of recent app events and errors, the newest 500 per account, cleaned of personal details before it is stored. We use it only to find and fix problems.

We use this data only to run Clabbers: to sync your study, show the leaderboards, run Versus matches, check daily-puzzle answers, send the reminder emails you ask for, and fix problems. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or share it for anyone else's purposes.

5. What other players can see

Daily puzzle leaderboards

  • Leaderboards are visible to anyone signed in to Clabbers, including guests, and past days stay viewable.
  • You appear on them only if you have set a display name. They show your display name, rank, score or number solved, time taken, the number of words you found or played, and weekly totals, streaks and days played.
  • Without a display name you are not listed, but your result still counts when ranks are worked out.

Versus

  • Players in a match see each other's display name (or "Player" if none is set), live scores and results. In Boggle Versus, everyone sees the words each player found once the match ends.
  • The other players' apps also receive your internal account ID, a random code that does not contain your email address or name.
  • Each player's match history keeps a record of the match, with the display names, scores and placings of everyone in it.

6. The Contact page

If you send us a message from the Contact page, we receive what you submit: the topic, your message, a reply email address if you give one (it is filled in from your account, and you can change or clear it), the platform you are on (for example android or desktop), and any images you choose to attach.

  • For a Bug Report, a box to attach a diagnostics report is ticked by default; untick it to send your message without one. The report holds technical information only: the app version and build, the platform, sync status, and a list of recent app events and errors, with account IDs shortened and sign-in tokens removed. On the website it is a short reference instead (version, a session code and the first characters of your account ID).
  • The message is sent to our support inbox (Gmail) by email through Resend. It is not stored in the Clabbers database. It stays in our inbox until we delete it; ask us and we will.
  • Our server uses your IP address, in memory only, to limit each connection to a few messages an hour.

7. Emails we send

  • Account emails from Supabase, delivered through Resend: sign-up confirmation, password reset, and confirmation when you add or change your email address.
  • Due-card reminders, only if you turn on "Email me when I have cards due" in the website's account settings (off by default). A reminder contains your display name, how many cards are due, a link to study, and a one-click unsubscribe link, and is sent at most once a day.
  • Our replies, if you contact us.

We do not send marketing email.

8. Downloads and updates

  • Word lists that are not built into the app (for example NWL2023 on phones) download from GitHub when you ask for them. GitHub sees your IP address and the app's standard browser details; the app sends nothing else.
  • The Windows app checks GitHub for updates when it starts and installs them when you quit. The update tool sends GitHub a random installation ID it creates for staged roll-outs, along with your IP address. The portable Windows build does not check for updates.
  • The desktop app can optionally write memory hooks or deck drafts with an AI model. It is off by default. If you set it up, the words, definitions or text involved go to the service you configure (a model on your own computer, or a provider you choose), under that provider's own terms.

GitHub's handling of this data is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Service providers

These companies process data on our behalf, only to provide Clabbers:

  • Supabase: sign-in, and the database that holds account and study data, hosted in Singapore.
  • Our web server: the clabbers.app website and API run on a server in Singapore.
  • Resend (which delivers through Amazon SES): sends account emails, reminder emails and Contact page messages.
  • Google: "Sign in with Google", and the Gmail inbox where Contact page messages arrive.
  • GitHub: word-list downloads, desktop app updates, and hosting for this page.

Your data may be processed outside your country, mainly in Singapore, and by these providers in the places where they operate.

10. IP addresses and logs

  • Like any website, our server sees your IP address when you connect. It uses it briefly, in memory, to stop abuse (rate limits), and does not store it in our database.
  • Our server's error logs can contain internal account IDs, never passwords.
  • Supabase and Resend keep their own service logs, which can include IP addresses and email addresses, for the periods their own policies set.

11. Browser storage on the website

The website sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for your sign-in session, your appearance and display settings, and unfinished daily-puzzle answers, so they survive a page reload. The desktop app keeps the same kind of information in its own data folder.

12. Device permissions

  • Android: internet access; notifications, asked for only when you turn study reminders on; and two permissions the notification library needs to keep reminders scheduled after a restart (run at start-up, keep awake). A storage library Clabbers uses also lists fingerprint and biometric permissions; Clabbers never uses them.
  • Clabbers does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts. It reads files only when you pick them (for example an image for the Contact page or a list to import), and it writes to the clipboard only when you tap a copy or share button.

13. How long we keep data

  • Account and study data on our server: until you delete your account or ask us to.
  • Guest accounts: until deleted. A guest whose session ends (for example after signing out or clearing the browser) cannot sign back in, but its data stays on the server until it is deleted; email us with any details you have and we will remove it.
  • Deleted cards: restorable for about 30 days; after that only a record that the card was deleted remains.
  • The website's technical log: the newest 500 events per account.
  • Contact messages: in our inbox until we delete them.
  • Service providers' logs and backups: for the periods their own policies set.
  • Data on your devices: until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Uninstalling the desktop app keeps its data folder (on Windows, %APPDATA%\Clabbers); delete that folder to remove it.

14. Deleting your account and data

In the app or on the website

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Type your email address and the word delete, then confirm with your password (or by signing in with Google again); a guest account types delete only. The account and everything our server keeps for it (listed in section 4) are deleted at once, and so is your sign-in. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you cannot use the app, email clabbersapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or with any details that identify a guest account). You do not need to reinstall anything. We delete the account and its data the same way within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What stays after a deletion

  • Other players' Versus histories keep their record of matches they played against you, with your display name, score and placing.
  • Contact messages in our inbox, until we delete them; ask us to.
  • Service providers' logs and backups, until they expire.
  • The copy of your study data on each of your devices, which the app keeps so you can still study offline, until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

15. Security

  • All connections between the apps, the website and our servers use HTTPS.
  • In our database, each account's study data is walled off from other accounts by database rules that the server's own database access cannot bypass. The only data shared across accounts is what section 5 describes.
  • Passwords are handled only by Supabase, which requires at least 8 characters with upper- and lower-case letters, a digit and a symbol, and refuses passwords known from data breaches.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

16. Children

Clabbers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

17. Your choices

  • Use the apps without signing in, and nothing about your study leaves your device.
  • Leave the display name blank to stay off the leaderboards.
  • Leave reminder emails off, or unsubscribe from any reminder with one click.
  • Untick the diagnostics box on a bug report.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, at clabbersapp@gmail.com. You may also have rights under the privacy laws where you live; contact us to use them.
